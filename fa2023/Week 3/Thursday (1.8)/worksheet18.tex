\subsection{Short Answer Questions}
\begin{newquestion}
	For each question, provide your work and answer.
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item The linear transformation \(T\) is defined by \(T(\vec{x}) = \begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 9 & 2\end{bmatrix}\vec{x}\).
			\begin{enumerate}
				\item What is the domain of the transformation?
				\item What is the codomain of the transformation \(T\)?
				\item What is the range of the transformation?
			\end{enumerate}

		\item Given the following information, construct the standard matrix \(A\) of the transformation defined by \(T(\vec{x}) = A\vec{x}\).
			\[T\left(\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 3\end{bmatrix}\right) = \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 5 \\ 2\end{bmatrix}, \quad T\left(\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 1\end{bmatrix}\right) = \begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0 \\ 1\end{bmatrix}\]
			What is the domain of the transformation? What is the range?

		\item Is it possible to construct a linear transformation \(T \colon \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m\), where \(T(\vec{x}) = A\vec{x}\), and not all of 
			the rows of \(A\) have pivots, yet for any vector \(\vec{b} \in \mathbb{R}^m\) there exists at least one vector \(\vec{v} \in \mathbb{R}^n\) 
			such that \(T(\vec{v}) = \vec{b}\)?

		\item Give a geometric description of what the linear transformation \(T\) does if its standard matrix is \[\begin{bmatrix}2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}.\]
			What is the domain of \(T\)? What is its codomain? What is its range?

		\item[\textit{Extra}] Notice that the previous standard matrix could be thought of as a sequence, or \emph{composition}, of two other linear 
					      transformation. Write the standard matrix that describes each, and then see how they relate to the previous standard matrix.
		
		\item Provide a nontrivial solution to the equation \(T(\vec{x}) = \vec{0}\) given that for the linear transformation 
			\(T \colon \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^2\), \[T\left(\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 4 \\ 1\end{bmatrix}\right) = T\left(\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ 0 \\ 1\end{bmatrix}\right) \ne \vec{0}.\]
	\end{enumerate}
\end{newquestion}
